Simple tools for sharing and discovery.

Loop keeps the social experience focused: post photos and short videos, find content that fits your interests, save moments and talk privately.

Photo and short-video sharing

Share image posts and short videos with a caption. Loop’s current upload limits are 10 MB for photos and 50 MB for videos up to 60 seconds.

A For You feed with more context

The feed ranks recent posts using a mix of freshness, topic interests learned from your viewing and interaction history, and optional city or region matches. It also applies topic and creator variety so one subject or account is less likely to dominate consecutive positions.

Loop does not use likes or dislikes as ranking signals. It uses the actions you take — such as viewing, bookmarking and commenting — to understand what topics may interest you.

Bookmarks, comments and conversations

  • Bookmarks: save posts to a private collection.
  • Comments: join the conversation with comments of up to 100 words.
  • Direct messages: start a private one-to-one conversation from a profile.
  • Notifications: see meaningful interactions such as comments and bookmarks.

Simple view counting

Loop counts a photo view after a signed-in viewer spends at least three seconds on its individual post page. A video view is counted after at least six seconds of actual playback, or after the full clip if it is shorter. The same signed-in viewer is counted once per post, and creators’ own views are excluded.
