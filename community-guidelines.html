Keep Loop thoughtful and welcoming.

Loop works best when people can share a moment and join a conversation without being targeted, harassed or overwhelmed by harmful content.

Be respectful

Speak to people with care. Do not use comments or messages to threaten, harass, bully, intimidate or repeatedly target another person.

Do not post hateful or abusive content

Do not post slurs, demeaning attacks, threats or content that encourages violence against people or groups. Loop uses a basic blocked-word filter for comments, but automated filtering is not perfect and does not replace human judgment.

Share responsibly

Only upload photos and videos you have the right to share. Respect other people’s privacy. Do not publish someone’s private contact details, precise location or other sensitive information without permission.

Keep conversations useful

Comments are limited to 100 words. Avoid spam, repeated promotions, misleading links and content unrelated to the post. Direct messages should be welcome and respectful; stop contacting someone if they ask you to stop.

Moderation and current limitations

Loop is an early-stage product. Its current comment filter is a basic blocklist and may miss harmful language or incorrectly flag acceptable words. Not all reporting, blocking or appeal tools are available yet. Do not rely on the filter as a guarantee that every post or message is safe.

Help keep the community considerate.

Use Loop thoughtfully and treat other members with respect.
