A little less noise. A little more meaning.

Loop is a lightweight social space for sharing everyday moments, discovering things that interest you and talking with people — without like or dislike buttons.

Why Loop exists

Social platforms can make every post feel like a competition. Loop takes a simpler approach: share a photo or short video, add a caption, discover other people’s posts, bookmark what you want to revisit and join conversations.

Loop is designed around the content and the people behind it, rather than a public like score.

What you can do on Loop

  • Build a minimal profile with a profile picture, display name and bio.
  • Share photos and short videos.
  • Explore a For You feed shaped by recency, topics and optional location signals.
  • Bookmark posts and comment with a 100-word limit.
  • Visit profiles and start private one-to-one conversations.

Local discovery, with choice

Loop can use a city and region to help surface locally relevant posts. Location is optional. You can enter it manually, use browser-assisted detection, or skip it. Loop stores city and region labels for recommendations rather than keeping your precise GPS coordinates.
