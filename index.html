GOOD TO KNOW

About Loop

What is Loop?

Loop is a lightweight social app for sharing photos and short videos, discovering posts, bookmarking content and having conversations.

Does Loop use likes or dislikes?

No. Loop does not have like or dislike buttons. You can bookmark posts, comment and message other users.

How does the For You feed work?

The feed can consider recent posts, topic interests and optional city or region matches. Your own activity helps personalize what you see.

Do I have to share my location?

No. Location is optional. You can enter a city and region manually, use browser-assisted detection, or skip it. Precise coordinates are not stored by Loop.
